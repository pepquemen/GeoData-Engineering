about=Data Engineering Toolkit adds a panel docked at the bottom of the QGIS window that profiles every field of a vector layer at once: type, nulls, distinct values (and candidate keys), min/max, mean, median, standard deviation, percentiles, sum, range and outliers, plus a distribution sparkline per field. Inspired by the Data Engineering view in ArcGIS Pro, with a key difference: everything is connected to the map. Click a statistic (nulls, min, max, outliers) or a chart bar to select those features (Ctrl to add, Ctrl+Shift to remove), open them in the attribute table, or see your current map selection overlaid on the chart. Profiling runs in the background in a single pass with bounded memory, so it stays usable on large datasets such as cadastral layers, and results are refreshed when the layer is edited. Works with numbers, text, booleans, dates, datetimes and times. Light and dark themes, English and Spanish.
